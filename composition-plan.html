Element Content purpose Normal-flow baseline Chosen technique and why Containing block / stacking context Fallback or disengagement Required test
Figure credit Says who made the image and under what licence. Sits under the image as a normal paragraph. Absolutely positioned at the bottom left of the image, 40rem and up. Keeps the credit on the image instead of taking up a line. Containing block is the media wrapper around the image. Sits at --layer-overlay, 30. Under 40rem it drops back to normal flow between the image and the caption. 320, 768, 1280. Pointer and stacking.
Story guide Jump links to the four sections. A normal block at the top of the page. Sticky with a 1rem offset, 52rem and up. Stays visible while you scroll. Containing block is its grid area in main. The viewport does the scrolling. Sits at --layer-sticky, 20. align-self:start keeps it from stretching. A stretched item kills sticky. Not sticky under 52rem. Sticky at page and section ends. 200% zoom, keyboard focus, pointer.
Pull quote Pulls a quote out of the body text. Full-measure blockquote in normal flow. Shifted -2rem at the inline start, 52rem and up. Breaks out of the text column. Containing block is the section. Sits at --layer-raised, 10. Under 52rem it sits inline. The shift matches the grid gap. It stops at the guide's edge. 320, 768, 1280. Content growth and stacking.
Hero clip Opening image for the first section. Plain full rectangle. Polygon clip with one diagonal. Nothing important sits in the cut area. No containing block role. The clip makes its own stacking context. No z-index needed. No clip-path support means the full rectangle shows. Turn off clip-path. 320, 768, 1280. Check stacking.
Portrait wrap Image for the tools section. Block image above the text. Floats inline-start, 52rem and up. Text wraps to a circle that matches the rounded edge. The section contains the float. It uses display:flow-root. No shape support means text wraps to the box. No float under 52rem. Turn off shape-outside. 320, 768, 1280. Content growth.
Paw accent Decoration. Empty alt keeps it out of the accessibility tree. None. The page works without it. Absolutely positioned bottom right of the guide, with pointer-events:none. Sits over the Sources link. That is where pointer safety gets tested. Containing block is the guide. Accent at --layer-base, 0. The links stay in normal flow. None needed. Pointer, keyboard focus, stacking.

Support and fallback record

References checked 26 September 2026.

Feature MDN reference Support Fallback on this page
position:sticky CSS position Baseline in current browsers. Falls back to static. The guide sits in normal flow.
clip-path CSS clip-path Baseline for basic shapes like polygon. Gated by a support test. Without it the image stays a full rectangle.
shape-outside CSS shape-outside Baseline in current browsers. Gated by a support test. From 52rem up the float stays. Text wraps to the box.
shape-margin CSS shape-margin Comes with shape-outside and shares its support. Text meets the circle with no gap.
@supports CSS @supports Baseline in current browsers. A browser that cannot read the at-rule skips the block. The enhanced styles do not apply.
